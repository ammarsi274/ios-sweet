drg: performance counters, quieter AKS log (efficiency plan Phase 0/1)

bsd/kern/drg_perf.c, own code, measures only: every 60 s (boot-arg
drg_perf=N, 0 = off) one "DRG PERF:" report in the kernel log with the
clock of the reporting core (PMU cycles against the generic timer), the
user LSE/LDAPR emulation count and busiest PCs, the top processes by CPU
time in the period, eMMC traffic and time in the driver, and frames
copied to the panel with their cost. Hooks: sleh.c after an emulated
instruction, drg_emmc.c strategy, AppleCLCD vsync present.

DrgKeyStore.cpp: log each succeeding selector twice and every failure,
instead of every call (triald alone printed hundreds on the console).
