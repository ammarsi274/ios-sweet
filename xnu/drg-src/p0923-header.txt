drg: IOSurface rows aligned to 256 bytes for the Adreno (patch 0923)

Boots #60-#64: backboardd drew with the GPU at 24 frames/s without a single
error, yet the screen stayed black. Its 1125x2436 display surfaces had
4544-byte rows (1136 px; our sel 13 told IOSurface.framework 64-byte row
alignment). In TWRP, Nokia's Adreno driver rendered nothing into a buffer
described with a 1136 px pitch (bench -gbufstride 1136: 0 of 2,806,784
words written) and every texel in place with 1152 px, the stride Nokia's
gralloc itself picks for width 1125. 256 bytes (64 px) makes CoreAnimation
choose 4608-byte rows. The framebuffer present and every client use the
surface's own bytes-per-row, so nothing else changes.
