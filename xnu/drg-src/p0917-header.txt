drg: screen brightness as AppleARMBacklight (patch 0917)

backboardd's CoreBrightness finds no backlight service (boot logs:
IOPropertyMatch backlight-control -> none) and only drives a service
that conforms to AppleARMBacklight. Publish one with backlight-control
and IODisplayParameters brightness {min 16, max 4095, value}; a
"brightness" property write sets the PM660L WLED strings 0-2 and syncs
them (HMD leds-qpnp-wled.c, research/reports/backlight.md), over the
SPMI arbiter's channel only when the ownership table gives it to EE 0.
