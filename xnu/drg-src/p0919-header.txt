drg: power off and restart, backlight off with the display (patch 0919)

Boot #53: iOS shut down for a 0 % battery reading (6 LowBatteryLog
reports) and every shutdown panicked "Halt/Restart Timed Out" after 30 s:
nothing set PE_halt_restart. DrgPlatformExpert now restarts as the shim
does (IMEM restart_reason 0, PSCI SYSTEM_RESET, PS_HOLD) and powers off
as HMD msm-poweroff.c/qpnp-power-on.c do (PON PS_HOLD reset type 4 on
PM660 and PM660L, PSCI SYSTEM_OFF, PS_HOLD), writing the PON only when
the SPMI ownership table gives it to EE 0. Panics keep their screen.

The panel is an LCD: DrgFramebuffer's "display off" painted black but
left the WLED at level 2047 (boot #53 drew 400-900 mA). Display off/on
now also switches the WLED module (DrgBacklight.cpp, patch 0917).
