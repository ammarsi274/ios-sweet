drg: battery from the PM660 fuel gauge and charger (patch 0916)

DrgBattery published a static 100 % once. It now reads the PM660's
monotonic SOC (with its copy), battery voltage, current, temperature,
USB plug-in and charger state over the SPMI arbiter's observer
channels (plain reads, nothing written, no fuel gauge SRAM), every
30 s and the plug-in bit every 5 s, and publishes them as an iOS power
source. Registers and conversions from HMD's qpnp-fg-gen3.c, fg-reg.h,
smb-reg.h and smb-lib.c (research/reports/battery.md). A bad sample is
dropped; until the first good one the static values stay.
