drg: scale the presented iPhone X frame onto the Nokia panel (patch 0910)

Boot #30 (touch working) showed the 1125x2436 frame cropped to the
1080x2280 panel: 22 columns lost at each side and 78 lines at the top and
bottom (status bar clock, home indicator). AppleCLCD now maps every panel
pixel to the presented pixel at its centre (tables built once) and samples
each layer through that map, blending and converting as before. DrgHID
maps a panel position to the same fraction of the presented display.
